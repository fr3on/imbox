# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Imbox to be a good experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

Examples of behaviour that makes the community better:

- Being kind and patient with people, especially people who are new
- Respecting other points of view and experiences
- Giving feedback that is specific and useful, and taking it well
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, apologising, and learning from them
- Keeping the discussion on what is best for the project and the people who use it

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or the text of their mail, without their permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be thought inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

This applies in all the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and anything else run in its name) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for making these standards clear and will take fair action when someone breaks them. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may ban someone, temporarily or for good, for behaviour they consider harmful.

To report a problem, use the private report form on the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) and say that it is about conduct. Reports go to the maintainers only. They are handled in confidence, and the maintainers will not name the person who reported.

If the person you would report is a maintainer, say so in the report, and the other maintainers will handle it. While the project has only one, you can also report to GitHub.
