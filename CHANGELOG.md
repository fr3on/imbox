## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-16

First release.

### Added
- Mail from your Gmail, Outlook and any IMAP mailbox in one place, for one owner, on a server you run.
- The Screener: new senders wait for your yes or no. The Imbox holds mail from people, and Updates holds newsletters and receipts.
- Reply Later, Set Aside, Bubble Up, bundles, labels, collections and workflows that act on mail on their own.
- Search with filters, contact pages, a page for every attachment, and an away mode.
- A calendar with Google Calendar, subscribed feeds and email invitations.
- An optional AI assistant, with the model of your choice, that acts only when asked. Mailboxes and an API for outside AI agents.
- A Stalwart mail server connection, passkeys and two-factor sign-in, a browser extension and a desktop app.
- Tracking pixels removed on arrival, remote images hidden until you show them, tokens and passwords stored encrypted, and nightly backups that can be encrypted to a public key.
